# Closing validations and the ethylene reference repair

The four initial closing tests below use the original candidate. Its F/Q weights 0.5/0.5, exchange-response scale 0.9 and zero energy offset are unchanged. The protocol is committed before new scoring labels in `94d2312`. All electronic preparation, molecular optimization and numerical verification run on the verified Mac Studio. Laptop work is limited to editing, transfer, scalar-result inspection, plotting and document rendering.

1. **Exact Hessian assembly.** Dense residual completion is vectorized and frozen auxiliary values avoid scalar jet allocation. The full two-state Criegee public energy, gradient and Hessian are bitwise identical. A cold two-thread call takes 397.37 s versus 418.04 s, a single-run 4.9% reduction; peak RSS increases from 1.06 to 1.19 GB. Other host tasks were active, so this is a modest implementation improvement, not a cost-scaling claim. Ten meaningful algebra/spin/legacy-contract tests pass.
2. **Anchor choice.** Eight actual reference preparations at carbon displacements 0.01/0.03 bohr compare gaps at identical final geometries. Original target states are retained. With physical CSF membership transported by occupied/virtual orbital overlap, maximum changes are 0.04694 eV for Criegee's pi state and 0.02990 eV for formaldehyde. Fresh symmetry selection changes the Criegee space from 178 to 13 CSFs and shifts its pi gap by 0.263–0.273 eV. That operation defines a different model and must not occur silently during a trajectory. Integer-index-only membership is retained as a separate diagnostic. An initial reporting bug selected the first state rather than the matching state ID; the corrected reports and original unaltered eigensystems are retained.
3. **Physical minima.** Formaldehyde and acetaldehyde are optimized on the final anchored energy with analytic gradients, followed by full analytic Hessians and independently reconverged scalar-energy checks. Chemical-coordinate projections assign selected modes. Geometry references are CC3 or CCSDR(3); experimental frequency references are explicitly anharmonic fundamentals, compared to unscaled model harmonic curvatures. No physical error is converted into a numerical validation pass.
4. **Prospective states.** Formaldehyde, ethylene and bright butadiene give OO-CID errors +0.319, +1.182 and +0.410 eV, versus matched native-GFN2-orbital TDA errors +0.684, +3.533 and +2.345 eV. The new MAEs are 0.637 and 2.187 eV. All three identities, spin-pure states and restarts are resolved with no DFT/TDA/SCC call during prepared evaluation. Ethylene remains outside the old 0.5 eV accuracy criterion; this extension does not establish universal accuracy.

The new native-valence state seeds and their preparation amendment are disclosed in `preparation_amendment.json`. Formaldehyde retains its already completed qualified reference-transition seed. A bibliographic approximate formaldehyde vertical value was seen after its prediction; this is a prospective fixed-model assessment, not a strict blinded claim. Native valence seeds remove a TDA representability condition for ethylene and butadiene, without fitting their energies. Every completed SCF, stationary state and operator is reused. The unnecessary unfinished butadiene reference-TDA attempt is stopped after its completed ground/embedding checkpoints are preserved.

`qualification_summary.json` distinguishes completion of the four experiments, numerical correctness, and physical accuracy. `portable_examples/` contains all three new checksummed runtime bundles. `results/` contains every selected matrix report, anchor control, runtime check, mode file and derivative certificate. Larger electronic arrays remain on the verified remote host with an inventory of their hashes.

For that original candidate, the scientific limitations remain explicit: the small new set exposes an ethylene error, the physical frequency comparison includes anharmonicity, and small anchor tests are not a guarantee for large distortions, CT or Rydberg states. The manuscript is updated around these measured limits; no new parameter is tuned to make the extension pass.

The diagonal full-DFT-complement self-energy repairs the confined F reference before the unchanged 50/50 F/Q average. Mixed native/bath Coulomb vertices screen the original frozen CID interaction. Exchange lambda stays 0.9; orbital gaps are not scaled and the energy offset remains zero. There is no molecule or state-family branch.

All 23 exposed states in 21 molecules converge and lie within 0.5 eV: MAE 0.2169504 eV, maximum 0.4820709 eV. The nine pi states give MAE 0.2829598 eV; fourteen n-pi states give 0.1745157 eV. All labels are exposed development. The 21 states not used to select eta give MAE 0.1917573 eV, without a blind-validation claim.

The full corrected Hessian takes 10.15 s on the verified MAC-STUDIO; two independent scalar-energy directions give maximum Richardson discrepancies 1.73e-11 Ha/bohr and 6.56e-8 Ha/bohr². These are numerical derivative checks, not a physical frequency comparison. `portable_corrected/Ethylene/` is a complete checksummed model and restart bundle.

All corrected states pass fresh-process energy/spin/restart audits, with no runtime DFT imports, TDA solve or SCC call. The corrected ethylene LINK cold start, accepted-only restart and displaced-trial rollback pass. `results/bath_eta060/` contains all molecule reports, matched TDA results and runtime certificates. `empirical_bath_summary.json` reports every state and identifies the two calibration states.
