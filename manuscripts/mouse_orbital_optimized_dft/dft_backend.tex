\subsection{Full-DFT implementation of the orbital response}
A separate reference implementation reuses molecular UHF/UKS energies,
Fock matrices and analytic density-response kernels from PySCF.\cite{sun2020pyscf}
Fixed-occupation unrestricted $\Delta$SCF and common-orbital ROKS solve
orbital stationarity with Newton--Krylov steps, retaining negative
curvature without a TDA calculation or MOM reassignment. ROKS uses a residual trust region and retained-target recovery;
final stationarity and response release every nonredundant rotation (SI). The
coherent ROKS CSF is a pure singlet, while its common-orbital
$2E_M-E_T$ functional energy remains approximate. Neither an energy
offset nor semiempirical kernel parameters enter this implementation.

The analytic nuclear Hessian includes the complete noncanonical
energy-weighted density, coupled common-orbital response, moving
Becke-grid derivatives, AO derivatives through third order and LibXC
vxc/fxc. Water and formaldehyde checks cover HF, LDA, PBE0 and CAM-B3LYP;
DFT Hessian--vector errors are below $5.1\times10^{-9}$ Ha/bohr$^2$.
Cached grid intermediates and the revised solver preserve complete
Hessians within $2.4\times10^{-13}$ Ha/bohr$^2$. The SI documents
retained-target recovery and derivative certificates. These small-basis
implementation checks establish neither spectral accuracy nor a speed
advantage over MOM, and do not extend the semiempirical accuracy ledger.
